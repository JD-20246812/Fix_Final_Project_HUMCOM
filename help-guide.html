Help & Guide

How to use this website

  1. Open Service.
  2. Choose a district from the list on the left. Press Enter or Space to open it.
  3. Pick a place to see its address and available time.
  4. Choose a date and time on the right, then press Make appointment.

Using the keyboard

  • Tab moves to the next control. Shift+Tab goes back.
  • Enter or Space presses a button.
  • Esc closes the settings panel.

Settings (gear button)

Use the gear button to make text bigger, switch to high contrast, or use a more readable font. Your choice is remembered.
