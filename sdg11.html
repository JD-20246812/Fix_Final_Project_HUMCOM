SDG 11: Sustainable Cities and Communities

Target 11.7 asks for safe, inclusive and accessible spaces, and Target 11.3 calls for inclusive urbanisation. Health services that every district can reach make a city more inclusive and resilient.

In Baguio City, growing numbers of students, workers and tourists put pressure on services. A simple directory shortens the path from "I need help" to "I found help".
